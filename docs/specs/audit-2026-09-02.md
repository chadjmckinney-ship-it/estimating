## P0 — actively broken

### 1. ~~Grade beam writes are dead. Every one returns 500.~~ FIXED

`app/routers/grade_beams.py` read `slab.estimate_id` and `t.estimate_id`;
neither column existed after sql/034. `db.commit()` ran before the crash, so
the beam was written, the client got a 500, and the takeoffs never resynced.

Fixed: `section_id` throughout; `_resync_section_takeoffs` recalcs the SECTION
then rolls up; the resync moved **before** `db.commit()`;
`tests/test_grade_beams_api.py` (10). The `_reinforced_type()` note: LBJ's GB 1
and GB 2 carry no loose bar, so 500 LF of one correctly moves concrete and not
steel — the test asserts both behaviours.

### 11. ~~`recalc_section` did not flush unless it recalculated pours.~~ FIXED

Found behind #1. The `db.flush()` sat inside `if pours:`; under `pours=False`
the callers' pending ORM writes were invisible to the raw-SQL takeoff readers.
Equipment survived only because `_super_days` carried its own flush — **one
flush in one reader rescued one of three consumers and disguised the hole in
the other two.** Fixed by moving the flush out; pinned twice.

### 2. ~~No takeoff endpoint rolls the job up.~~ FIXED — structurally

PROVEN: one `PUT /api/wall-runs/bulk` moved the section $162,920.41 →
$237,719.77 and left the estimate at $162,920.41.

`refresh_pour_costs` is the ONLY writer of `section.calc_total_cost`, and
`costing._roll_up_parent` rolls the estimate up from inside it.
`tests/test_write_paths.py` walks every mutating endpoint.

### 3. ~~A single-row PATCH leaves every takeoff stale.~~ FIXED

PROVEN on columns: one type 38 → 400 left the section **$436,826.42 light**.
Every router's `_recost` now calls `recalc_section`.

Derived-supervision assemblies (mono slab, columns) are never flagged.
`tests/test_audit_small_fixes.py`.

## P2 — wrong now, small money

### 7. ~~Columns buys METAL CHAIRS where the line says SLAB CHAIRS.~~ FIXED

`forming.py` asked for `"CHAIRS"` and got METAL CHAIRS 2.5" ($45) by sort
order; the line is labelled SLAB CHAIRS ($27). Now asks for `"SLAB CHAIRS"`
first, as the mono slab always did. `test_columns.py`'s APP figures moved:
allocated −$18.00, tax −$1.49, total **172,300.84 → 172,281.35**, sale
203,315.00 → 203,291.99, $/column 2533.8359 → 2533.5493. **LBJ columns will
move −$19.49 on its next recalc** — expected, and correct.

### 8. ~~The vapor barrier fallback lands on black site poly.~~ FIXED

`_find_material(db, "10 mil", "20")` resolved to `POLY 10 mil 20 x 100 Black`
($105, `site_accessories`), and with `default_vapor_barrier_material_id = 0`
that was the live path for any section that had not named a roll.

Fixed three ways: `_find_material` takes `category=`, and
`vapor_barrier_fallback` searches **`vapor_barrier` only** (lands on
`10 mil 20 x 100`, $110); a section that reaches the fallback says so —
`vapor_barrier_source` = section / default / fallback on the mono-slab totals
endpoint, and the picker's hint reads *"no company default set — using 10 mil
20 x 100. Choose a roll here, or set the default in Settings."* in warning
colour; and a section with poly, no roll, no default and nothing in the
category is **unpriced** (`vapor barrier — none chosen and no company
default`), not $0.

While there: `rebar_material`, `vapor_barrier`, `vapor_tape` were computed by
the service and **dropped by `MonoSlabTotals`** — the fourth instance of the
schema-drop class. Carried now.

### 9. ~~`load_stored_equipment` serves confident zeros.~~ FIXED

Rebuilt drivers from six summary columns; served `"0"` for paving's 9,537 LF
